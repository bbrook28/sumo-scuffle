About the game

The gong sounds and the food starts falling. Four hungry sumo wrestlers have 45 seconds to eat as much as they can, and they are not above shoving. Every sumo is a ball: roll around the ring, grab onigiri, sushi and the legendary golden chanko, and charge your rivals into the walls to knock their snacks loose. Bounce off taiko drums like a pinball. Fall behind and the game helps you catch up, and the last 10 seconds score double, so nobody is ever out of it.

Sumo Scuffle is made for people who don't usually play games. The Dojo teaches everything step by step with no clock. Tilt your phone to roll and tap anywhere to charge, or drag your sumo with one finger, or use the classic thumb stick.

Features

  • Four ways to play: Feast, King of the Ring, Keep-Away and Sumo Bowling, or mix them in a Party
  • Pass-and-play for 2 to 4 players on one phone or tablet
  • Climb the banzuke in a career mode, from Jonokuchi to Yokozuna
  • A new Daily Challenge every day, with Game Center leaderboards
  • Surprise moments: runaway onigiri boulders, rice rain, gusts and the golden sumo
  • Slow-motion replays of every bout's best slam, ready to share
  • The Dojo: step-by-step lessons with no clock, for players new to games
  • Tilt controls, one-finger controls, or a thumb stick with a left-handed layout
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking

About the developer

Sumo Scuffle is made by Ben Brook, a game designer and programmer who studied at SCAD (Savannah College of Art and Design). It grew out of a SCAD student project Ben designed and programmed with Hagar Baruch (UX design), Ethan McKelvin (graphic and UI design) and Zohaib Qureshi (characters and objects), and was rebuilt from the ground up for phones and tablets.
