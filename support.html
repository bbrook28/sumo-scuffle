Report a problem or ask a question

Email benbrookgames@gmail.com. Tell us your device, what happened, and what you expected. We read every one.

On GitHub? You can also open an issue.

New to the game?

Open the Dojo from the main menu. Its lessons teach everything step by step, with no clock: moving, eating, shoving, slams, power-ups, and every mode.

Controls

Joystick: drag on the left side to roll, tap SHOVE to charge. One-handed: turn on "Follow my finger" in Settings, then drag your sumo around and tap to charge. Left-handed players can swap sides in Settings.

Game Center

Daily Challenge scores go to Game Center leaderboards when you're signed in to Game Center on your device. The LEADERBOARD button appears once you are.

Progress

Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.

Privacy

Sumo Scuffle collects no personal data. You can choose to share anonymous play stats, and change that any time in Settings. Read the privacy policy.
