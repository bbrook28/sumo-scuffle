Sumo Scuffle Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Sumo Scuffle does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress (level, unlocks, missions, career, settings, Daily Challenge scores) is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your Daily Challenge scores can be posted to Game Center leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; Sumo Scuffle never sees your Apple ID.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example, when a new Daily Challenge is ready). They are created and delivered on your device; nothing is sent to us.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the system share sheet opens with a picture of your best moment. Where it goes is up to you.

Children

Sumo Scuffle is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.
