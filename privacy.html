Sumo Scuffle Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

Sumo Scuffle does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. The only thing that ever leaves your device is optional, anonymous play statistics, and only if you say yes.

Optional anonymous play statistics

From version 1.2, after your first bout the game asks once whether you'd like to share anonymous play statistics. If you tap Yes, it sends:

  • a random ID created when the game is installed (stored scrambled, never tied to you, your Apple ID or your device),
  • the game version and platform,
  • what happens in play: bout results (mode, arena, scores, how close it was, the rivals' skill), which Dojo lessons and steps you finish or skip, which control scheme you use, and how long you play.

No names, email addresses, contacts, location or advertising IDs are collected. The statistics are used only to balance the game and improve the lessons. They are never sold, used for advertising or used to track you across apps.

They are processed by TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany), a privacy-focused analytics service, on our behalf. If you tap No, nothing is sent. You can change your answer at any time in Settings (Share anonymous play stats), and turning it off stops all sending.

What stays on your device

Your progress (level, unlocks, missions, career, settings, Daily Challenge scores) is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your Daily Challenge scores can be posted to Game Center leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; Sumo Scuffle never sees your Apple ID.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example, when a new Daily Challenge is ready). They are created and delivered on your device; nothing is sent to us.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the system share sheet opens with a picture of your best moment. Where it goes is up to you.

Children

Sumo Scuffle is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children; the optional statistics above are anonymous and sent only if someone on the device says yes.
